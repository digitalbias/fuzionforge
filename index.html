The problem

Your tools don't talk to each other. So you do.

Your CRM, your scheduling, your invoices, your bank and your books all hold a piece of the story. None of them share it. That gap gets filled by you.

Every job gets typed in three times

Estimate, schedule, invoice, books. Same customer, same numbers, different people, and every handoff is a chance for a mistake.

You can't see what's working

You find out a job lost money a month after it's done. Which clients, crews and lead sources actually make you money is a guess.

Everything routes through you

Every question about a client, a payment or a schedule ends up on your phone. A week off means a week of catching up.

You didn't start a business to be its data-entry clerk. And you shouldn't need to be a software expert to run a well-organized company. Good systems should be built for the owner, not the other way around.

What changes

Imagine running a business that runs without you in the middle

Enter it once

Your CRM, scheduling, invoicing, bank feeds and accounting software (QuickBooks, ERPNext and others) share data automatically. A signed estimate becomes a scheduled job and a posted invoice, with no re-typing.

Know your numbers

Reports built for how your business makes money: profit by job, client and lead source, across one company or several. Decide with facts, not gut feel.

Let AI handle the busywork

AI that categorizes transactions, drafts follow-ups, summarizes calls and flags what needs your attention, with you approving anything that touches money or customers.

Get the tool that actually fits

When nothing off the shelf works, we build it: web apps, internal tools, client portals and mobile apps. You own the code and the documentation.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I have to replace the software I already use?

Almost never. Our first move is connecting what you already have. We only recommend replacing a tool when it's genuinely holding the business back, and we'll explain why.

What does it cost?

It depends on scope, which is why we start with a free call. After that you get a written plan with a clear scope and price before any work starts, so there are no surprise invoices.

How long before I see results?

We deliver in small pieces and build the highest-value fix first, so you see results early instead of waiting for one big launch.

Is my data safe?

Yes. We use least-privilege access, keep your data in your own systems wherever possible, and can sign a business associate agreement (BAA) for healthcare work.

Do you work with businesses outside Utah?

Yes. We're based in Lehi, Utah and work remotely with businesses across the US.

What's the difference between Fusion Forge and Dialed In Trades?

Same company. Dialed In Trades is the name Fusion Forge uses for its work with landscaping, irrigation and lawn care businesses.
